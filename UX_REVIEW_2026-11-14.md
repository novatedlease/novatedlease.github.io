# Calculator UX review, round 3 (2026-11-14)

Reviewed as a first-time, non-financial, possibly stressed reader. Branch
`codex/provider-guide-completion`, preview on port 4340.

**How it was tested:**
- Every Guided step was captured at 390 px and 1440 px, in light theme, in four states:
  - empty;
  - after pressing Next with nothing filled;
  - a realistic EV quote taken through to results;
  - the "just exploring" path.
- Advanced mode was captured at both widths.
- Screenshots are in the session scratchpad (`ux/`).

Everything in `UX_FIXES_HANDOFF_2026-11.md` is done or was dropped by you (for example, "Not sure" on
What you're comparing). None of it is raised again here.

Items are ranked by how much they affect a reader's decision. Effort: S = under an hour, M = a few
hours.

## A. Trust in the result (do these first)

**1. "Lease: payments" hides the running costs (S).**
- **Where:** results → "Show how we got this", and Advanced Summary (`SummaryView.tsx` lines 204
  and 244).
- **Problem:** The figure is the total take-home pay reduction, so finance and running costs after
  tax are combined (`takeHomeImpactPerPay` × pays). The cash side lists "Cash: running costs
  $21,838" as its own line. A novice sees running costs only on the cash side and concludes the
  lease figure leaves them out, or that the comparison is rigged.
- **Fix:** relabel it "Lease: take-home pay reduction (finance and running costs, after tax)". If
  that's too long, use "Lease: payments incl. running costs" with a tooltip.

**2. Guided never shows the take-home pay change (M).**
- **Problem:** The figure every quote leads with is the reduction in take-home pay, for example
  "$542 per fortnight" on the Paywise quote seen in the archive. The results speak only in 5-year
  totals, so the reader can't connect our answer to the number on their quote.
- **Fix:** under the headline, add "Your take-home pay: about $X less per fortnight (in your quote's
  period)", then "Compare this with the take-home figure on your quote."
- **Bonus:** if the two differ a lot, the reader has probably entered something wrong. That makes it
  the cheapest error check we could add.
- **Data:** the engine already has the figure.

**3. Lost super isn't counted, and its size isn't shown (M).**
- **Problem:** When the reader says super is worked out "After lease salary", the About-you hint
  warns it "can add up to many thousands of dollars". The results then only say "Your super may also
  be reduced under this setup" in grey text. The headline saving ignores that loss.
- **Fix:** show the estimated super shortfall over the lease next to the headline, for example
  "Not counted above: about $X less super over 5 years". Advanced already works out super guarantee
  effects. Alternatively, subtract it from the headline, which is your call on the method.

**4. The Your alternatives step has no sanity checks (S–M).**
- **What happened:** While testing, I typed a $400 loan establishment fee into "Loan monthly
  account fee". It was accepted silently. That added $24,000 to the loan's cost, and the headline
  read "$59,197 better off than a car loan" on a $59,500 car.
- **Fix:**
  - **Hint:** "Monthly account-keeping fee, often $0–$15. Not the one-off establishment fee."
  - **Warnings, the same style as the other steps:**
    - monthly fee over about $30;
    - loan rate under 2% or over 25%;
    - market value at lease end above today's value;
    - current car's yearly running costs over about $15,000.

**5. The answers list changes what the reader typed (S).**
- **Residual:** entered "$16,860, Including GST", but the review list shows "Residual (ex GST)
  $15,327". To a novice that looks like a wrong number. Show it as typed: "$16,860 including GST".
- **Lease start:** shows "2026-12-15". Show "15 Dec 2026".

**6. Unanswered questions show up as real values (S).**
- **When:** a saved or partial draft reaches Review with gaps.
- **Problem:** the list shows "$0", "? years", "0 km" and "Petrol/diesel/hybrid," (with a stray
  comma, when no vehicle type was chosen). "$0" reads as an answer.
- **Fix:** show "Not answered" in the missing style, with its Edit link.

## B. Friction and cognitive load

**7. Income: the key condition is only in the tooltip (S).**
- **Problem:** "Use the yearly figure before tax, as it is before the lease" is inside the ⓘ. The
  visible hint asks for "after deductions … the taxable income line on your tax return". Many people
  don't know that figure, and people already salary packaging may enter their reduced figure.
- **Fix:** visible hint: "Before tax and before this lease. Your taxable income from your last tax
  return is best."

**8. Payment step: the amount comes last (M, worth testing).**
- **Problem:** The reader is asked how the payment is shown (period, GST, Smart/MillarX) before
  they've found the payment. With a quote open, people find the number first and then read what's
  printed around it.
- **Fix:** put "Finance (lease) payment" first and the period and GST questions under it. The money
  box's period suffix can follow the period answer as it does now.

**9. Phone: the "Still to answer" bullets are misaligned (S, visual bug).**
- **Where:** Payment step after pressing Next, at 390 px. Long items wrap and the bullet sits beside
  the second line. The third item ("Is your lease with Smart Leasing or MillarX?") shows no bullet.
- **Likely cause:** the item's inline link and the list-marker styling. Check `.gd-missing` list CSS.

**10. Advanced: two different "Electricity" fields (S).**
- **Problem:** the running-cost list has "Electricity" (the packaged budget at the ATO 5.47c/km
  shortcut), and there's also an "Electricity" section for your real charging cost. Readers will
  enter the same thing twice or in the wrong place.
- **Fix:** rename the running-cost one "Electricity budget in the lease".

**11. Advanced: misplaced and unclear fields (S).**
- **NSW Health employer share:** it sits between Servicing and Registration in the running-cost
  list. Move it to Financials.
- **"Financed amount reported in your quote … leave as the pre-calculated figure unless…":** no
  novice can act on this. Suggested hint: "Leave as is unless your quote shows an amount financed.
  Used only for the interest rate."

**12. "Lease: electricity gain / loss" is jargon (S).**
- **Where:** results breakdown and Advanced.
- **Fix:** "Charging: lease budget vs your real cost", keeping the tooltip.

## C. Small copy and consistency

**13. The page's help text names a section that doesn't exist (S).**
- **Problem:** the "How to use this calculator" text in `new-site/src/pages/calculator/index.astro`
  says "Assumptions we made for you". The results call it "What we assumed (n)".
- **Fix:** align the help text with the UI.

**14. The quote path lists no assumptions (S–M).**
- **Problem:** Explorer results list "What we assumed". The quote path shows only the residual and
  interest notes. Readers can't see unstated assumptions such as current tax rates or the cash buyer
  paying the same running costs.
- **Fix:** add a short "What we assumed (n)" on the quote path as well, so the method is visible.
  It builds trust without adding steps.
