# How to read a Prosperity Smart Drive novated lease quote

<!-- quote-review:begin -->
*Checked against 1 quote example; latest layout reviewed Nov 2026.*
<!-- quote-review:end -->

This is the layout of [Prosperity Smart Drive](https://www.prosperitysmartdrive.com.au/), part of Prosperity Advisers Group. Its quote names itself only in its "Smart Drive Administration Fee" line, and it isn't part of Smart (Smartsalary and Smart Leasing): for a Smart quote, see the [Smart Leasing guide](/start-here/how-to-read-smartleasing-quote/).

The Smart Drive layout we reviewed puts **Lease Payment (Fixed)** under a heading marked **Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)**. For running costs, it separately shows **Less: Employer Claimed ITC**, the GST credit.

<!-- provider-traps:begin -->
## What catches people out on a Prosperity Smart Drive quote

- **Running costs:** If the heading is “Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)”, choose Including GST and enter the costs as printed.
<!-- provider-traps:end -->

| # | Guided field | Look for on the quote | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | FBT base value | **FBT Base Value** | The value as printed, including GST. |
| — | Drive-away price | It may not be printed as one total | Use Guided's price helper or your dealer's full drive-away price. |
| 3 | Lease term | **Lease term (months)** | Use the months shown. |
| 6 | Residual | **Residual (inc. GST)** | Choose *Including GST*. |
| 7 | Finance payment | **Lease Payment (Fixed)** under **Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)** | If “Lease Payment (Fixed)” sits under “Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)”, choose Including GST and the printed period. |
| 9 | Running-cost GST | **Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)** and **Less: Employer Claimed ITC** | If the heading is “Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)”, choose Including GST and enter the costs as printed. |
| 10 | Lease management fees | **Smart Drive Administration Fee** and **Less Your Salary Packaging Administration Fee** | Add “Smart Drive Administration Fee” and “Salary Packaging Administration Fee” if both are charged. |
<!-- provider-table:end -->

Do not use the total salary deduction as the finance payment: it also includes running-cost budgets.

<!-- provider-missing:begin -->
## If a figure isn't on your quote

- **Months deferred:** Can’t find months deferred on your Prosperity Smart Drive quote? Choose “Not sure” (we’ll assume 2) unless told otherwise.
- **Documentation fee:** Can’t find a documentation or establishment fee on your Prosperity Smart Drive quote? Leave this at $0 unless yours lists one.
