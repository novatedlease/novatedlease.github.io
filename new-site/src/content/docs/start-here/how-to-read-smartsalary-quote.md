# How to read a Smartsalary Smart Drive quote for Guided mode

The Smart Drive layout reviewed in November 2026 puts **Lease Payment (Fixed)** under a heading marked **Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)**. For running costs, it separately shows **Less: Employer Claimed ITC**, the GST credit.

![Schematic Smartsalary Smart Drive quote layout with empty value boxes and the labels used by Guided mode](/assets/images/quote-guide/smartsalary/layout.svg)

*Original label-only schematic. It is not a customer's quote or a Smartsalary form. Your layout may differ.*

| Guided field | Look for on the quote | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FBT base value | **FBT Base Value** | The value as printed, including GST. |
| Drive-away price | It may not be printed as one total | Use Guided's price helper or your dealer's full drive-away price. |
| Residual | **Residual (inc. GST)** | Choose *Including GST*. |
| Finance payment | **Lease Payment (Fixed)** under **Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)** | Enter the lease payment, choose the printed period, and choose *Including GST*. |
| Running-cost GST | **Motor Vehicle Deductions (inc GST)** and **Less: Employer Claimed ITC** | Choose *Including GST* for the heading, then answer *Yes* to the GST-credit question. Enter running-cost amounts as printed. |

Do not use the total salary deduction as the finance payment: it also includes running-cost budgets.
