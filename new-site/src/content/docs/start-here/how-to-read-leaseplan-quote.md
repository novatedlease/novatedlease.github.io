# How to read a LeasePlan / Ayvens novated lease quote

<!-- quote-review:begin -->
*Checked against 1 quote example; latest layout reviewed Aug 2025.*
<!-- quote-review:end -->

This numbered example shows a LeasePlan / Ayvens quote layout we reviewed. Match each marker to the table, then check the period and GST label on your own quote.

<!-- provider-traps:begin -->
## What catches people out on a LeasePlan / Ayvens quote

- **Drive-away price:** Can’t find a drive-away total? Ask for the full on-road price including GST. The quote we checked had no residual to work it out from.
- **Documentation fee:** If “Finance Documentation” is monthly, put it in management fees. Enter any one-off “Security Interest” fee as the documentation fee.

## Numbered field guide

<!-- provider-table:begin -->
| # | On the quote | In Guided |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | FBT Base Value | FBT base value, including GST. |
| 5 | Security Interest | If “Finance Documentation” is monthly, put it in management fees. Enter any one-off “Security Interest” fee as the documentation fee. |
| 7 | Finance Payment under Monthly Budgets | Finance payment per month. Confirm the GST basis on your own quote; this sample does not state it on the line. Do not use Monthly Payment (ex GST), which includes the package budgets. |
| 9 | Maintenance, Tyres, Fuel, Registration, Insurance, Misc Operating Costs and Roadside Assistance | Running cost budgets per month. |
| 10 | Management Fees (including FBT Fee) | Lease management fees per month. |
<!-- provider-table:end -->

The sample page does not show a clearly labelled drive-away price, lease term, deferral or residual. Ask for the full on-road price including GST; the residual helper may not work without a printed residual. Use another page of your quote or ask the provider for the other figures. This example is older than the others, so check current LeasePlan / Ayvens layouts carefully.

## If a figure isn't on your quote

- **Months deferred:** Can’t find months deferred on your LeasePlan / Ayvens quote? Choose “Not sure” (we’ll assume 2) unless told otherwise.
- **Residual:** Can’t find a residual on your LeasePlan / Ayvens quote? Choose “I’m not sure” and we’ll use the ATO minimum.
